Privacy notice

Updated October 18, 2026. Spotter is a developer assessment and route-planning demonstration.

Information used

When you search or analyze a trip, Spotter processes the addresses or locations you enter, departure time, load weight and sampling interval. The browser sends these to our backend to produce the requested analysis. There are no user accounts or saved trip histories in this application.

Services involved

In live mode, address searches and route coordinates are sent to Google Maps Platform. Weather sample coordinates and forecast times are sent to Open-Meteo. Load weight is used by Spotter's risk calculation. Google Maps loads directly in your browser and may receive your IP address, browser information and map interactions. Google Fonts also supplies web fonts. Railway hosts the deployed service and may process request metadata.

Google services are subject to the Google Privacy Policy. See also Open-Meteo's terms and privacy information and Railway's privacy notice. Synthetic demo mode uses OpenStreetMap tiles instead of Google map tiles.

Storage and logs

Spotter does not persist trips to a database. Weather forecasts are cached in process memory for up to 30 minutes for resilience. Application logs record request references, status, timing and failure categories rather than address query strings or API keys. Hosting and third-party services have their own logging and retention policies. Do not enter confidential customer addresses in this assessment demo.

Questions

Contact the developer who provided your demo or assessment link with questions about this deployment. Include a request reference if reporting an error; do not send API keys.
